Brand identity guide · Dark mode

01

The logo

On dark ground the whale keeps its blue field, but its outer ring switches from navy to Splash Sky so it never sinks into the background. The logo is always cropped as a circle or a rounded square.

Primary · on dark
Circle · on navy
Circle · on blue

Clear space

Keep a margin around the logo of at least half its diameter. No text or other elements inside that zone.

Don'ts

Don't stretch, rotate or recolor the whale, don't use the navy ring on dark backgrounds — it disappears — and don't place the logo on grounds that fight with its blue.

03

Typography

Fredoka for headlines, Nunito for body — unchanged. On dark ground, headlines are near-white and accents use Whale Blue or Splash Sky; navy text is never used here.
