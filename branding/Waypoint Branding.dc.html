Brand identity guide

01

The logo

The Waypoint whale is friendly, round and optimistic. Its thick navy outline is core to the identity — every brand element inherits it. The logo always lives inside its blue field, cropped as a circle or a rounded square.

Primary · on light
Circle · on navy
Circle · on blue

Clear space

Keep a margin around the logo of at least half its diameter. No text or other elements inside that zone.

Don'ts

Don't stretch, rotate or recolor the whale, don't remove the navy outline, and don't place the logo on backgrounds that fight with its blue.

Typography

Two rounded typefaces that echo the whale's curves: Fredoka for headlines, Nunito for body copy. Both are free on Google Fonts.
